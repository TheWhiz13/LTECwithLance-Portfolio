## Portfolio launch post

I have been building LTECwithLance as a product ecosystem, not just a collection of apps.

The model is simple: **app for engagement + toolkit for implementation + training for adoption + evidence for improvement.**

The current portfolio includes **Should I AI This?**, **AI Governance Check**, **Cybersecurity Situational Awareness**, **Cybersense: Ready Now**, **Mission 20**, **The Last Archive**, and **LTEC Venture Scientist**.

The common thread is human judgment. Each product gives people a concrete decision to make, then supports reflection, documentation, practice, or improvement.
