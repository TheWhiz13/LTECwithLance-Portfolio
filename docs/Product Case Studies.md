# Product case studies

Seven app and toolkit projects showing applied learning technology, responsible AI, AI governance, cybersecurity, experiential design, narrative strategy, and controlled R&D.

The application source repositories are currently private. This public portfolio documents the products, live experiences, design logic, toolkit connections, and evidence of work without exposing private application code.

## Case study frame

Each case study uses the same structure:

- **Problem:** the practical learning, governance, security, or research need.
- **Design:** how the experience creates decisions, feedback, reflection, or controls.
- **What was built:** the app, toolkit, training asset, or operating framework.
- **Value:** why the product matters to users or organizations.
- **Evidence:** the visible proof of work, including live products, packaged resources, or operating controls.

## Should I AI This?

**Domain:** Responsible AI  
**Live app:** https://wisecheck.ltecwithlance.com/  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Toolkit:** Responsible AI Toolkit for College Faculty

### Problem
Faculty, staff, and small teams often need a fast way to decide whether generative AI is appropriate for a specific task, especially when privacy, accuracy, attribution, and accountability are unclear.

### Design
The product turns responsible AI from an abstract policy concern into a pre-use decision. Users review task purpose, information sensitivity, verification needs, human responsibility, and consequences before using AI.

### What was built
A transparent decision-support web app that produces plain-language guidance without presenting itself as legal advice or compliance certification.

### Value
Creates a repeatable habit of checking risk before a user pastes, prompts, or uploads.

### Evidence
A public companion app paired with editable toolkit materials for syllabus language, assignment directions, disclosure, verification, privacy, attribution, redesign, and training.

## AI Governance Check

**Domain:** AI governance  
**Live app:** https://governance.ltecwithlance.com/  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Toolkit:** Practical AI Governance Toolkit

### Problem
Small and mid-sized organizations need AI governance, but enterprise-scale frameworks can be difficult to operationalize at the department, college, nonprofit, government, or SMB level.

### Design
The product begins with a plain-language description of the intended AI use, separates inherent risk from governance readiness, and applies deterministic routing so AI context analysis cannot override the final classification.

### What was built
A practical intake and routing tool for assessing sensitivity, AI authority, human review, consequences of error, safeguards, and escalation needs.

### Value
Helps organizations apply more governance only when the risk, data, authority, or consequences justify it.

### Evidence
The app connects to the **Know • Own • Assess • Control • Monitor** governance model and a toolkit containing policy, RACI, risk appetite, intake, impact assessment, vendor review, monitoring, incident response, training, and framework crosswalks.

## Cybersecurity Situational Awareness

**Domain:** Cybersecurity  
**Live app:** https://cyber.ltecwithlance.com/  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Toolkit:** Cybersecurity Situational Awareness Toolkit v2.0

### Problem
Traditional security awareness can emphasize remembering rules rather than practicing judgment in realistic situations.

### Design
Learners inspect suspicious artifacts, make decisions, receive feedback, practice in the Decision Lab, and complete randomized assessment pathways.

### What was built
A scenario-based cybersecurity learning platform with lessons, twelve security domains, randomized questions, final assessment, certificates, progress tracking, XP, badges, and administrative visibility.

### Value
Builds practical security judgment through repeated evidence review and decision practice.

### Evidence
A public learning platform paired with implementation resources for training delivery, incident response, access review, vendor risk, backups, continuity, tabletop exercises, and quick-reference support.

## Cybersense: Ready Now

**Domain:** Cybersecurity  
**Live app:** https://detect.ltecwithlance.com/  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Toolkit connection:** Companion reinforcement for the Cybersecurity Situational Awareness Toolkit v2.0

### Problem
Users need short, repeatable practice for recognizing manipulation and knowing what to do when they may already have clicked, shared, paid, or exposed information.

### Design
Short scenarios surface warning signs and attacker tactics, then provide scoring, explanations, readiness feedback, and recovery-oriented guidance.

### What was built
A rapid scenario trainer with challenge prompts, tactic recognition, readiness profiling, feedback, and Incident Coach guidance.

### Value
Provides practical decision repetitions between formal training sessions and adds a recovery path when the user may already have made a mistake.

### Evidence
A public companion product linked to the same cybersecurity implementation toolkit as the structured Cybersecurity Situational Awareness platform.

## Mission 20

**Domain:** Experiential learning  
**Live app:** https://mission20.ltecwithlance.com/  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Toolkit:** Mission 20 Financial Decision-Making Toolkit

### Problem
Financial decision-making and resource allocation can become abstract when taught only through explanation rather than lived constraint.

### Design
A fixed $20 constraint forces participants to choose, prioritize, compare, and explain. The learning cycle moves through **Understand → Plan → Choose → Explain → Compare → Transfer**.

### What was built
A social challenge app with missions, submissions, voting, reactions, comments, following, leaderboards, badges, and recurring challenges, paired with facilitator resources.

### Value
Makes resource allocation, opportunity cost, value assessment, decision-making under constraints, and financial agency visible through experience.

### Evidence
A public app plus a facilitator toolkit with challenge archetypes, educational mapping, instructional depth, debriefing, evidence of learning, safety and equity guidance, and multiple implementation formats.

## The Last Archive

**Domain:** Narrative strategy  
**Live app:** https://archive.ltecwithlance.com/  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Product type:** Standalone experiential product

### Problem
Some questions about values, scarcity, prioritization, and technological stewardship are better explored through consequential choices than through direct instruction.

### Design
Across seven in-game days, the player manages a failing digital archive, allocates scarce resources, consults a council of artificial intelligences with distinct philosophies, and makes irreversible preservation choices.

### What was built
A branching narrative strategy experience in which the ending and final transmission reflect accumulated decisions rather than a single correct answer.

### Value
Uses story, scarcity, and consequence to make values and priorities visible.

### Evidence
A public interactive experience with a complete narrative loop. No packaged toolkit is claimed in the current LTEC toolkit catalog.

## LTEC Venture Scientist

**Domain:** Venture R&D  
**Source repository:** Private  
**Product type:** Controlled internal research platform

### Problem
Early venture ideas are easy to overvalue when research, falsification, economics, risk, and approval are blended into one informal process.

### Design
The system separates analytical roles, evidence sources, experiments, decisions, approvals, and audit history. External action remains outside the automated research loop.

### What was built
A controlled venture-research backend with opportunity scouting, research analysis, falsification, economics, risk analysis, experiment design, venture judgment, evidence provenance, and human approval gates.

### Value
Creates a disciplined research layer for testing opportunities without autonomous spending, outreach, bidding, trading, purchasing, advertising, or external commitment.

### Evidence
Working integrations and controls for SEC EDGAR, USAspending, read-only SAM.gov intelligence, structured agent outputs, approval states, and auditable evidence lineage.

## Common product pattern

Across the portfolio, the recurring design pattern is:

**App for engagement + toolkit for implementation + training for adoption + evidence for improvement**

The products differ in audience and purpose, but each is built around a concrete decision, structured human judgment, and a way to make the resulting practice visible.
